## The Problem with Typical Ad Management

Traditional ad management often fails for three reasons:

1. **No real audit.** Campaigns run on gut feel or surface metrics. Budget leaks to poor audiences, duplicate keywords, and untracked conversions.
2. **Disconnected from discovery.** Ads drive clicks, but the brand isn't visible or trusted in AI search. Lead quality suffers.
3. **Weak follow-through.** Leads land in a generic CRM or get lost because there's no automated qualification, booking, or nurturing tied to the ad source.

You end up paying for volume instead of value. Many operators see high spend but flat or declining pipeline.
