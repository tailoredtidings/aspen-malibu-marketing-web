## Why Retainer Pricing Varies by Revenue Tier

Your marketing infrastructure should scale with your business — not with an agency's appetite for billable hours. We tier pricing by annual revenue because:

- A $800K practice has different complexity than a $15M multi-location group
- Platform usage, automation volume, and AI agent throughput scale with operation size
- Ad spend management (when applicable) correlates with revenue tier

Both engagements use the same four revenue tiers. The difference is who runs the system: you (Growth Accelerator) or our senior team (Bespoke Service).

**Why are setup fees so high?**

Setup deploys your entire revenue infrastructure — CRM, automations, AI agents, integrations, and reporting — in 14 days. It is a systems build, not a website refresh. The setup fee covers senior implementation hours that would cost $15K–$30K at hourly agency rates.

**Is there a contract minimum?**

Yes. 3-month minimum on all engagements, with 30-day written notice to cancel after that. No exit fees. Your website and content are yours — we hand over keys cleanly.

**Can I see pricing without a sales call?**

Yes. Growth Accelerator pricing is fully published and self-service. Bespoke and website pricing are available instantly in our [estimator](/#estimate). We confirm scope on a free 30-minute call but you see the real number first.

**How does ad management pricing work?**

Managed ads are billed at 20% of actual monthly spend across Google, Meta, TikTok, and YouTube. No minimum spend required. You pay media costs directly to the platforms. The forensic ad audit ($3,995 for Growth Accelerator, included in Bespoke) identifies waste before we spend a dollar.
